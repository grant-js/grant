The users list shows a user's email at a glance: the address of their Email sign-in method is the second line of the user card header, and the users table has an Email column (after Name, and like any column it can be hidden or moved; someone with a saved column order finds it at the end). Users who sign in only with GitHub or Google show nothing there; the sign-in method badges are unchanged.
